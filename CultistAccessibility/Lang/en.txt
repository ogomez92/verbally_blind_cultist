# Help
Help.Logo.Title = Logo and title quote
Help.Logo.1 = Press any key to continue to the main menu.

Help.Menu.Title = Main menu
Help.Menu.1 = Up and down arrows move between buttons and text. Home and End jump to the first and last item.
Help.Menu.2 = Enter activates the focused button. Left and right arrows change sliders and choices. Enter on a text announced as a link opens it in your browser.
Help.Menu.3 = Escape closes an open panel such as settings, credits or mods.
Help.Menu.4 = [[UI_CONTINUE]] resumes your saved game. [[UI_BEGIN_GAME]] starts a new one. [[UI_PURGE_SAVE]] deletes your save after a confirmation.
Help.Menu.5 = DLC and Mods lists the extra legacies you can start and the installed mods.
Help.Menu.6 = In settings, choose a tab with Enter, then move down to its sliders. A key binding field waits for the new key after Enter.
Help.Menu.7 = {0} reads everything on the screen. {1} repeats the focused item.

Help.NewGame.Title = Choosing a legacy
Help.NewGame.1 = Up and down arrows move between the legacies on offer, their descriptions and the buttons.
Help.NewGame.2 = Enter on a legacy selects it; its description is then read in the text rows below it.
Help.NewGame.3 = [[LEGACY_START_NEW_GAME]] begins a new life with the selected legacy. [[LEGACY_BACK_TO_MENU]] returns to the main menu.

Help.GameOver.Title = The end
Help.GameOver.1 = The ending text was read when the screen opened. Up and down arrows read it again, line by line.
Help.GameOver.2 = [[LEGACY_ANOTHER_DESCENT]] chooses a new legacy. [[LEGACY_MAIN_MENU]] returns to the menu.
Help.GameOver.3 = The full ending text is also in the Story review buffer: Control plus left or right arrow to reach it.

Help.Crash.Title = Error screen
Help.Crash.1 = The game hit an error. Log Files opens the folder with the game's logs. Exit quits the game.

Help.Options.Title = Options menu
Help.Options.1 = The game is paused while the options are open. Up and down arrows move, Enter activates, Escape closes the options.
Help.Options.2 = The first items are the tabs: Enter on a tab shows its settings below it. Left and right arrows change a slider.
Help.Options.3 = Enter on a key binding waits for the new key.
Help.Options.4 = [[UI_RESUME]] closes the options. [[UI_SAVEEXIT]] saves and returns to the main menu. [[UI_RESTART]] asks for confirmation, then restarts this life from its beginning.
Help.Options.5 = [[UI_BROWSE]] opens the game's save folder in Windows.

Help.Board.Title = The table
Help.Board.1 = The table has three groups: Verbs, Cards and Controls. Left and right arrows switch group, up and down move inside it. Home and End jump to the first and last item.
Help.Board.2 = Enter on a verb opens its window. Enter on a card lists the verbs that can take it; choose one with Enter to put the card in.
Help.Board.3 = Controls holds your status, the stack cards button, the speed buttons, options and your character's name.
Help.Board.4 = {2} reads everything about the focused item: description, aspects and their meaning, slots. The lines also go to the Details buffer.
Help.Board.5 = {1} repeats the focused item. {0} reads the whole group.
Help.Board.6 = {3} reads your character and the status bar: health, passion, reason, funds.
Help.Board.7 = {4} reads the game speed, every busy verb with its time left, and cards that are decaying.
Help.Board.8 = {5} jumps to the next verb with results waiting.
Help.Board.9 = A running verb sometimes opens a slot and wants a card. This is announced, and the verb then says 'slot open' when focused. Open the verb, or press Enter on a card, to fill it before the time runs out.
Help.Board.10 = Game keys: Space pauses and unpauses, N normal speed, M fast forward, Tab stacks cards, Escape opens the options menu.
Help.Board.11 = Time runs while the game is not paused. Pausing with Space is the safest way to think.

Help.Window.Title = A verb window
Help.Window.1 = Up and down arrows move through the window: the story text, the slots, Start, results and Collect all.
Help.Window.2 = Enter on a slot lists the cards on the table that fit it. Choose one with Enter. If the slot is full, the first choice takes its card out.
Help.Window.3 = {6} or Backspace on a slot returns its card to the table.
Help.Window.4 = When cards are placed the window's prediction changes; the new recipe name is read, with 'ready to start' when Start will work.
Help.Window.5 = Enter on Start, or the game key S, begins the recipe. Enter on a result takes that card to the table; Collect all, or the game key C, takes them all.
Help.Window.6 = Enter on the story text reads it in full. Page up and page down, or left and right on the story text, turn the pages of a long story.
Help.Window.7 = Escape closes the window; cards left in slots of an unstarted verb return to the table.

Help.Mansus.Title = The Mansus

Help.Mansus.2 = Up and down arrows move between them. Enter turns a card over; the others fade away.
Help.Mansus.3 = Enter again takes the revealed card back with you and the dream ends.

Help.Picker.Title = Choosing from a list
